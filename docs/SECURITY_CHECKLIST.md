## 2. Emergency SOS Safety Exemption
- **Zero-Block Guarantees**: Emergency SOS requests (`POST /api/sos`) are explicitly exempted from CSRF blocking in [`server/src/middleware/csrf.ts`](file:///c:/Users/MAHEK/OneDrive/Desktop/hackathon/server/src/middleware/csrf.ts#L43) and maintain separate high-burst rate limiters to ensure life-safety distress calls are never dropped due to stale session tokens.
- **Fail-Safe Dispatch**: If email and SMS dispatch channels fail, a system-wide high-priority alert is broadcast to all security consoles and the client displays a persistent `Call 112` emergency hotline dialer.
